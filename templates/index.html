The Problem

Bridging the Communication Gap

Over 70 million deaf and hard-of-hearing people worldwide rely on sign language as their primary means of communication. Automated recognition can power real-time translation tools, assistive devices, and educational platforms — making the world more accessible.

Real-Time Recognition

Live webcam inference at interactive frame rates using optimised TFLite models and MediaPipe hand tracking.

Multiple Backends

ResNet CNN on Sign MNIST, MobileNetV2 on real photos, and a 98.9% accurate landmark MLP — choose your pipeline.

Web Deployment

FastAPI backend serving a modern web interface for upload-based image classification, accessible from any browser.

Edge Ready

TFLite export enables deployment on Raspberry Pi, Android, and resource-constrained hardware at the edge.
